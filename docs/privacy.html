RPM Gauge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

RPM Gauge ("the app") measures the RPM of model engines from the sound they make. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can measure engine speed from sound frequency. Audio is analysed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere. You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Data stored on your device

The measurement sessions you record (timestamps and RPM readings) are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They stay there until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. This data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No data sent over the internet
  • No sharing or selling of any information

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
